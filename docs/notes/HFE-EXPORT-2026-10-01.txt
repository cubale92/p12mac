P-12 SP-1200 HFE EXPORT v1.1.16

FIX:
- Removed the artificial eight-page fragmentation failure for HFE export.
- Rossum projects <= ~20 s are no longer forced to be re-chopped just because sound lengths cannot fit exactly into eight 64K pages.
- P-12 now packs intact sounds into as many 4-page vintage HFE images as required.
- No sample trimming and no automatic audio chopping.
- Existing Rossum .sp12 audio pipeline remains unchanged.

FORMAT NOTE:
Rossum has ~20 s / eight sound pages, while .HFE import is the vintage SP-1200 floppy-image format (four sound pages per HFE). A single HFE cannot represent the Rossum-only extra memory.
