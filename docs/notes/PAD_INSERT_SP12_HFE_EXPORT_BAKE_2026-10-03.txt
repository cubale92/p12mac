P-12 v1.0 - Pad Insert Bake for SP-1200 Export
Date: 2026-10-03

Change only:
- Active native P-12 PAD inserts are rendered into that pad's audio for .sp12 / HFE export.
- Intended for effects such as Auto Wah, Filter, EQ, distortion, modulation, delay/reverb, etc.
- Tune BF remains export-only and is baked into exported sample audio.
- Tune AT / Multi-Pitch / Multi-Level remain event/playback-side and are not baked as insert processing.
- Track inserts and master inserts are not baked into individual pad samples.

Everything else in the previous working build is preserved.
