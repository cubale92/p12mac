P-12 AIR / inMusic duplicate protection

- Detects AIR Reverb, AIR Compressor, Fabric XL and AIR/inMusic/Akai-labelled plug-ins.
- Shows AIR/inMusic family and install-path class in the VST browser.
- Adds AIR / inMusic / Akai vendor filter.
- Adds Preferred Copy Lock (enabled by default).
- USE THIS COPY both sets the preferred path and locks the identity to that path.
- Mixer-32 and CHORDS share the same preferred-copy guard.
- A non-preferred duplicate is rejected instead of silently replacing the selected copy.
- Standard VST3 path is identified as C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3; alternate copies are explicitly labelled.

This does not bypass or alter vendor licensing. It prevents P-12 from accidentally choosing a different duplicate installation after you select the activated copy.
