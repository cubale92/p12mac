P-12 Windows v1.1.2 - Rossum Safe Chop Pad Routing

FIXED
- Rossum Chop no longer blindly places slices on consecutive occupied pads.
- Every SP12 slice now has its own target-pad selector (A1-D8).
- AUTO FREE PADS starts at the chosen pad and skips loaded pads.
- A loaded pad that is itself >2.5 seconds is protected and cannot be overwritten by another sample's chop.
- After applying one chop, P-12 re-scans the project and automatically opens the next still-too-long pad for its own Rossum Chop pass.
- Duplicate slice destinations are blocked.
- Loaded but hardware-safe destination pads can still be chosen intentionally; P-12 asks for confirmation before replacing them.
- Pitch/Tune/Speed-Warp remain baked before the Rossum Chop screen, as in v1.1.1.

EXAMPLE
If A7 and A8 are both too long, chopping A7 will keep A8 protected. AUTO FREE PADS can use A7 plus another free pad (for example B1). After A7 is committed, P-12 opens A8 for its own chop instead of losing it.
