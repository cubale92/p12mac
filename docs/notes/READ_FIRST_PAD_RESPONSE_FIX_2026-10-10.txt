P-12 FULL15 — Larger pads / pad response update — 10 October 2026

Includes the complete previous Mixer 32 insert/transport update.
Extract to a fresh folder and run BUILD_FULL15_ORIGINAL_GUI.cmd to finish the
Windows build, then START_P12.cmd or the newly built installer.

Pad changes:
- Larger pads: minimum 160px wide and 230px high on the desktop main page.
  The bank retains all eight pads; the row scrolls horizontally when needed.
- Larger 30px option buttons, readable pad names and sample names.
- Load/remove tools at the top; reverse/slice/stem/warp and trim tools at
  the bottom, leaving the center available for playing.
- Tool rows and sliders never invoke the pad strike handler.
- Tool presses no longer move the entire pad; held-pad feedback is reserved
  for performance presses.
- Already-running audio takes a synchronous strike path, avoiding an async
  audio initialization wait for every hit. First use still initializes audio.
- Pointer capture and release/cancel cleanup prevent stuck pad repeats.
  Releasing one pad does not stop the other pad's held repeat.

Validation: browser checks for larger geometry, separated option rows,
20 synchronous ready-audio strike dispatches, no repeated initialization,
no strikes from options/trim sliders/right-click, and all eight pads present
at 1700px and 900px window widths. Integrated FULL15 preflight also passes.
The Rossum .sp12 encoder remains unchanged by protected-source hash.

This is the complete source/build package. Windows EXE compilation and
physical input-to-speaker latency cannot be measured in this Linux environment.
