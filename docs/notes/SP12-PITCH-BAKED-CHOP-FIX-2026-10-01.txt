P-12 Windows - Rossum SP12 Pitch-Baked Chop Fix
2026-10-01

FIXED
- Extract -> Rossum -> Chop now opens the waveform AFTER the pad's current Tune/pitch is baked.
- Speed-Warp is baked before Chop.
- Preserve-pitch Warp uses its rendered buffer before Tune is baked.
- Reverse is baked before Chop.
- Chop preview therefore sounds like the sample that will be written to Rossum.
- Slice count and the 2.5-second limit use the baked/tuned duration.
- APPLY FOR SP12 commits the baked buffer to the destination pads and resets Tune/Reverse/Warp on those pads to neutral, preventing double-pitching during final .sp12 export.
- Final .sp12 export and the Chop preflight now share the same rendering helper.

Example:
A 4.0-second sample at +12 semitones becomes about 2.0 seconds before the Rossum Chop screen is evaluated.
